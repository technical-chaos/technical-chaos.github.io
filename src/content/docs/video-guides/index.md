One page per video, laid out the same way:

1. **What you'll build:** the end result in one picture.
2. **The steps:** in the same order as the video, with timestamps.
3. **The exact settings:** VLAN IDs, subnets, ranges, rules and commands, ready to copy.
4. **Troubleshooting:** what went wrong on camera and how it was fixed.

All addresses and names are **lab or example values** from the video, not from a real network. Swap in your own.
